# Role Statement – AI Lead

As the AI Lead of Halal Chiang Rai, I was responsible for designing, developing, and evaluating the project's restaurant recommendation logic. My main contribution was implementing the rule-based recommendation approach to help users discover halal restaurants based on their search queries and preferences.

I worked on query interpretation, keyword matching, restaurant filtering, scoring, and ranking to generate relevant recommendations from the restaurant dataset. I also reviewed the recommendation workflow, identified limitations in the current implementation, and considered improvements to make the search experience more useful and accurate.

My responsibilities included documenting the AI logic, preparing test cases, and evaluating the recommendation results. I focused on ensuring that the system's actual capabilities and limitations were clearly explained rather than claiming functionality that had not been implemented.

## Reflection

### 1. Lessons Learned

Building Halal Chiang Rai with AI tools taught me that AI can make software development faster, but it does not replace software engineering knowledge. During this project, I learned that defining clear requirements is still important because AI-generated suggestions may not fully understand the project's goals. As the AI Lead, I worked on the rule-based restaurant recommendation logic, including keyword matching, filtering, and ranking. AI tools helped me explore solutions, understand unfamiliar code, and debug problems more quickly.

One thing that improved was development efficiency, especially when researching technical approaches and solving coding problems. However, one thing that became more challenging was verifying AI-generated code. Sometimes suggestions did not match our existing architecture or actual requirements. This reminded me that testing, code review, data privacy, and security must not be skipped. Team communication was also important because AI-related features needed to work consistently with the frontend and other project components.

### 2. My Position in the Software Industry in the AI Era

I believe I am still at the beginning of my software engineering career. In the AI era, developers need more than the ability to write code. They must understand problems, design appropriate solutions, evaluate AI outputs, and take responsibility for the quality and security of their work. I see AI as a development assistant rather than a replacement for software engineers. My goal is to become a developer who can use AI effectively while maintaining strong programming and problem-solving fundamentals.

### 3. My Plan

In the next year, I plan to gain practical experience through my internship, improve my frontend development skills, and become more confident in working with APIs, databases, testing, and team development workflows. I also want to improve my English communication and continue learning how to integrate AI features responsibly.

In five years, I hope to become an experienced software engineer who can independently design, develop, and maintain reliable applications. I want to be someone who understands both traditional software engineering and modern AI technologies, and can use them together to build useful products that solve real-world problems.
