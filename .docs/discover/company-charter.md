# Company Charter — Halal Chiang Rai

## 1. Project Purpose

Halal Chiang Rai is a web application that helps Muslim residents,
students, and visitors discover halal restaurants in Chiang Rai, Thailand. It
brings restaurant search and available details into one accessible,
Thai-and-English experience.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Help users browse restaurants by area and search by relevant keywords.
- Present available restaurant details and menu information clearly.
- Let users save favorite restaurants in the current prototype.
- Support Thai and English interfaces.
- Provide restaurant recommendations using the prototype's
  rule-based logic.
- Establish a clear foundation for evaluating and improving the product.

## 3. Project Scope

The current project is using a bundled restaurant dataset.
Its scope includes area-based browsing, keyword search, restaurant details and
menus where available, favorites, Thai/English language switching, and rule-based recommendations.

Backend services, live GPS or an interactive map, prayer-place discovery,
certificate verification workflows, community reviews, and restaurant-owner
or system-admin portals are outside the current prototype scope. They may be
considered as future work.

## 5. Responsibilities and Accountability

- **Project Owner:** Defines the vision, goals, and scope; gathers and
  prioritizes requirements; maintains the backlog; writes user stories and
  acceptance criteria; and reviews completed features and provides feedback.
- **Tech Lead:** Leads technical and architectural decisions; establishes
  coding standards; coordinates integration; reviews code and technical
  quality; and helps resolve technical issues.
- **AI Lead:** Designs and develops the local recommendation system, including
  query interpretation, filtering, scoring, and ranking; tests recommendation
  results; and documents limitations and possible future AI/LLM integration.
- **UX/UI Designer:** Researches user needs; designs user flows, wireframes,
  and layouts; creates visual assets; maintains design consistency; and
  collaborates with developers to support usability.
- **Tester:** Creates test plans and cases; performs functional and regression
  testing; reports bugs; and verifies features against acceptance criteria.

Each role is accountable for communicating progress, risks, and blockers
within its area and for coordinating work that depends on other roles.

## 6. Team Rules and Agreements

### Communication

- Communicate respectfully, clearly, and constructively.
- Share relevant progress, questions, risks, and blockers with the team in a
  timely manner.

### Meetings

- The team agrees on meeting times and cadence together; no fixed schedule is
  set by this charter.
- Use meetings to align on project work and decisions, and capture relevant
  outcomes and follow-up actions.

### Task Management

- Record work in the project backlog or task-tracking method agreed by the
  team.
- Keep priorities, ownership, status, and acceptance criteria clear enough
  for the team to coordinate and review progress.

### Collaboration

- Coordinate early when work crosses role boundaries or depends on another
  team member.
- Give and receive feedback professionally, and raise blockers before they
  prevent other work from progressing.

### Version Control

- Use the project's version-control system to track changes.
- Keep changes understandable and review them with the Tech Lead or relevant
  teammates before integration, following the workflow agreed by the team.

### Documentation

- Keep project documentation accurate and consistent with implemented
  behavior.
- Distinguish current prototype capabilities from proposed or future features.
- Document important decisions, testing outcomes, and known limitations where
  relevant.

## 7. Decision-Making Process

The Project Owner is accountable for product goals, scope, and priority
decisions. The Tech Lead leads technical decisions and explains relevant
trade-offs. The AI Lead leads decisions about recommendation logic, while the
UX/UI Designer and Tester provide input on usability and quality. The team
should seek agreement through discussion; when agreement is not reached, the
decision is made by the accountable role for that area and communicated to
the team.

## 8. Conflict Resolution

Team members should first discuss disagreements directly and respectfully,
using project goals, requirements, and evidence to clarify the issue. If the
team cannot resolve a product or scope disagreement, the Project Owner
facilitates a resolution. Technical disagreements are discussed with the Tech
Lead, with input from affected team members. Agreed outcomes and any resulting
changes should be communicated to the team.

## 9. Quality Assurance

- Define testable acceptance criteria for project requirements and features.
- The Tester verifies functionality and performs regression testing; feature
  owners support investigation and correction of reported issues.
- Review changes for correctness, usability, consistency, and alignment with
  the agreed scope before they are considered complete.
- Treat restaurant data and halal-related indicators carefully; do not imply
  official verification beyond the evidence available in the prototype.
- Report known limitations clearly, including the use of bundled data and
  local-only recommendation logic.

## 10. Working Principles

- Keep the Muslim User's needs and the project's stated purpose central.
- Prefer clear, maintainable solutions that fit the current prototype scope.
- Make decisions and limitations transparent.
- Respect each role's expertise and collaborate across disciplines.
- Prioritize accurate, understandable restaurant information and avoid
  unsupported claims.

## 11. Team Commitment

Each team member commits to fulfilling the responsibilities of their role,
communicating reliably, collaborating respectfully, and contributing to the
project's agreed objectives. The team will review and update this charter
together if project scope or working agreements materially change.
