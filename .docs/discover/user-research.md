# User Research Findings and Identified Pain Points

## 1. Research Overview

To understand users' needs, behaviors, and challenges when searching for Halal restaurants in Chiang Rai, our team conducted an online questionnaire survey.

The survey collected **25 responses** from people connected to Chiang Rai, particularly students and individuals in the surrounding areas.

The purpose of this research was to:
- Understand users' current restaurant-searching behaviors.
- Identify difficulties when finding Halal restaurants.
- Discover what information users consider important.
- Evaluate the potential usefulness of a centralized Halal restaurant platform.

**Research Method:** Online Questionnaire (Google Forms)  
**Number of Respondents:** 25  
**Research Area:** Chiang Rai, Thailand

## 2. Respondent Profile

The survey results show that most respondents have a connection to Chiang Rai.

- 84% study in Chiang Rai.
- 12% live in Chiang Rai.
- 4% have visited Chiang Rai occasionally.

Additionally, 96% of respondents identified themselves as students.

This indicates that the survey primarily represents the perspectives of students and people connected to the university environment.

## 3. User Behavior and Current Search Methods

### 3.1 Halal Food Consumption

Respondents reported different frequencies of consuming Halal food:
- 48% eat Halal food every day.
- 32% eat Halal food occasionally.
- 12% have never eaten Halal food.
- 4% have never eaten it but are interested in trying.
- 4% eat Halal food once a week.

### 3.2 Restaurant Search Channels

When searching for Halal restaurants in Chiang Rai, respondents commonly use multiple platforms:

| Search Channel | Responses | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Friends or people they know | 18 | 72% |
| Google Maps | 16 | 64% |
| TikTok | 11 | 44% |
| Facebook | 10 | 40% |

These findings suggest that users rely on several different sources rather than a single centralized platform.

### 3.3 Time Spent Searching

- 52% spend 5–10 minutes.
- 24% spend 10–20 minutes.
- 16% spend more than 20 minutes.
- 8% spend less than 5 minutes.

## 4. Identified User Pain Points

The survey revealed that 76% of respondents have experienced difficulties when searching for Halal restaurants.

The main problems identified were:

| Pain Point | Responses | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Difficult to find nearby restaurants | 15 | 60% |
| Incomplete or outdated restaurant information | 12 | 48% |
| Having to search across multiple sources | 9 | 36% |
| Unclear Halal information | 6 | 24% |
| Difficulty finding prayer rooms or facilities | 1 | 4% |

### Key Problems

**1. Difficulty Finding Nearby Restaurants**

Users have difficulty discovering Halal restaurants near their current location. Restaurant information may be scattered across different platforms, making location-based discovery less convenient.

**2. Incomplete or Outdated Information**

Users may encounter missing or outdated restaurant information, making it difficult to determine whether a restaurant is currently operating or whether its information is accurate.

**3. Information Scattered Across Multiple Platforms**

Users often need to switch between Google Maps, Facebook, TikTok, and recommendations from friends to gather sufficient information about a restaurant.

**4. Unclear Halal Information**

Some users have difficulty identifying clear Halal status or certification information when choosing a restaurant.

## 5. Information Users Need

When choosing a Halal restaurant, respondents considered the following information important:

| Information | Responses | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Halal status or certification | 17 | 68% |
| Menu and prices | 16 | 64% |
| Restaurant reviews | 13 | 52% |
| Distance | 12 | 48% |
| Opening hours | 9 | 36% |
| Parking availability | 3 | 12% |
| Prayer room availability | 1 | 4% |

The results indicate that Halal information, menu details, and prices are particularly important when users make restaurant decisions.

## 6. Desired Platform Features

Respondents selected the features they would find most useful in a platform that gathers information about Halal restaurants and Muslim-friendly places in Chiang Rai.

| Desired Feature | Responses | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Find nearby restaurants | 20 | 80% |
| Halal restaurant map | 18 | 72% |
| Menu and price information | 18 | 72% |
| Halal status information | 13 | 52% |
| Restaurant reviews | 13 | 52% |
| Restaurant filters | 5 | 20% |
| Nearby prayer rooms and facilities | 4 | 16% |
| Personalized recommendations | 1 | 4% |

Furthermore, 84% of respondents believed that the proposed platform would be very helpful, while 12% considered it helpful and 4% were unsure.

## 7. Additional User Feedback

Open-ended responses highlighted several additional needs:

- More complete restaurant coverage in Chiang Rai.
- Restaurant menus and detailed food information.
- Accurate locations and map information.
- Updated opening and closing hours.
- Restaurant contact information.
- Food delivery options.
- English language support and public transportation information.

These suggestions emphasize the importance of providing reliable, accessible, and useful restaurant information.

## 8. Proposed Solutions

Based on the identified pain points, the team proposes developing **Halal Chiang Rai**, a centralized platform that helps users discover Halal restaurants in Chiang Rai.

| Identified Problem | Proposed Solution |
|---|---|
| Difficult to find nearby restaurants | Browse restaurants by area and search by location-related keywords. |
| Incomplete or outdated information | Provide structured restaurant details and maintainable information records. |
| Information scattered across multiple platforms | Centralize restaurant information in one platform. |
| Unclear Halal information | Display available Halal status and verification information clearly. |
| Difficulty comparing restaurants | Provide menu, price, and other relevant restaurant details. |

## 9. Research Conclusion

The survey results indicate that users experience difficulties when searching for Halal restaurants in Chiang Rai, particularly in finding nearby restaurants, accessing complete information, and gathering information from multiple sources.

The findings also show strong interest in a centralized platform, with 84% of respondents expecting it to be very helpful.

Therefore, the team identified an opportunity to develop Halal Chiang Rai as a platform that improves restaurant discovery and makes important restaurant information easier to access.

**Research Limitation:** This research was conducted through an online questionnaire with 25 respondents, most of whom were students. The results may not represent all Halal food consumers or residents in Chiang Rai. The findings should be interpreted as initial user research rather than a representative survey of the entire population.
