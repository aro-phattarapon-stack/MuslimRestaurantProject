## 5. Qualitative Feedback Highlights

* **Most Liked Features:** Easy to use and clean/pleasant UI colors (`R02`, `R03`, `R04`, `R06`, `R09`, `R10`, `R11`, `R12`), Google Maps integration (`R01`), variety of restaurant choices (`R08`), and convenience for finding halal/local food near campus (`R06`, `R07`, `R09`).
* **Continued Usage Intent:** 11/12 respondents stated they would continue using the site; 1 respondent (`R01`) stated conditional future use pending 100% map pin accuracy.

## 6. Data Limitations Observed in Form Export

1. **Missing Participation IDs:** The `รหัสผู้ทดสอบ (Participation ID)` column was left blank across all 12 rows (preventing direct 1:1 mapping to `U01`–`U12` in `Task based testing (1).xlsx`), though 9/12 provided Student IDs.
2. **Potential Acquiescence Bias (`R05`):** Respondent `R05` (`SUS = 60.0`) rated both positive statements (`4`–`5`) and reverse-worded negative statements (`4`–`5` on Q2, Q4, Q6, Q10) high, which likely reflects straight-lining rather than dissatisfaction (their open-ended feedback reported no issues and intent to use).
3. **Empty Formula Row:** Row 14 in the Excel export contains an unpopulated `=AVERAGE(W2:W13)` formula producing `#DIV/0!`.
