## 1. Why Not (Reasons for Incomplete Real-User Task-Based Sessions)
- Our team conducted usability evaluations with **12 real target users** (`R01`–`R12` in the post-test SUS survey), consisting of **5 Muslim consumers** and **7 general consumers** aged 18–24.
- Among these participants, **2 real users (`U01` and `U02` in `Task based testing (1).xlsx`)** completed full moderated, timed task-based testing sessions across all 3 core tasks (`1st test` and `2nd test`) in addition to the SUS questionnaire.

- For the remaining **10 real users** (corresponding to the unpopulated `U03`–`U12` template rows in the task-timing sheet), due to tight project timelines and scheduling constraints for moderated in-person sessions before the final deadline, participants evaluated the live system independently and completed the 10-item SUS questionnaire and qualitative feedback (`Halal Nue Sud  (1).xlsx`) without per-task stopwatch tracking (and without logging Participation IDs on the form).

- In strict compliance with the course academic integrity policy (no fabricated data), we transparently report only verified metrics (`n = 2` timed task-based sessions; `n = 12` SUS responses) and supplement the remaining task-based coverage with team-executed E2E test cases, heuristic evaluations, and secondary evidence below.
